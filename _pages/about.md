Plant ecophysiologist with a Ph.D. in Biology and over a decade of experience conducting field-based research on gas exchange, water-use efficiency, and plant-environment interactions across arid and semi-arid ecosystems. Technical expertise in gas exchange systems, water potential, chlorophyll fluorescence, sap flow, soil moisture monitoring, leaf thermal regulation, and a wide range of functional traits measurements. Proven track record of peer-reviewed publications, stakeholder engagement, and interdisciplinary collaborations across ecology, agriculture, and conservation. Bilingual educator and communicator with deep experience in outreach and training.
