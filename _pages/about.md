Plant ecophysiologist with a Ph.D. in Biology and over a decade of experience conducting field-based research on gas exchange, water-use efficiency, and plant-environment interactions across arid and semi-arid ecosystems. Technical expertise in gas exchange systems, water potential, chlorophyll fluorescence, sap flow, soil moisture monitoring, leaf thermal regulation, and a wide range of functional traits measurements. 
Building on this scientific foundation, I spent over five years as a Western Region and Spanish Language Extension Associate with Cornell University’s Produce Safety Alliance. In this role, I bridged the gap between complex agricultural research and regulatory compliance, leading domestic and international training programs for growers, regulators, and educators. I specialize in the FSMA Produce Safety Rule, agricultural water quality, and Good Agricultural Practices (GAPs). As a bilingual educator, I have led the translation and delivery of critical curricula across the U.S. and Latin America, developing a wide range of educational resources and publications to support diverse stakeholder engagement in both English and Spanish. Proven track record of peer-reviewed publications, stakeholder engagement, and interdisciplinary collaborations across ecology, agriculture, and conservation.
